Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Summary

ImageRenamer does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data. Image processing and renaming take place locally on your Mac.

Files and folders

The app accesses only the images and folders you choose through the macOS file picker. Access is used solely to preview filenames and create renamed copies at the destination you select.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

ImageRenamer does not include analytics, advertising, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection SDKs.

Network access

The app does not upload your images or filenames to a server. Its core batch-renaming features work offline.

Children's privacy

Because ImageRenamer does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the new practices take effect.
